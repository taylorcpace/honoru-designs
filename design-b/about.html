01 — Ordering

QWhat sizes do your covers come in?

Four standard sizes: 8.5"×11" Letter (cover 9"×11.5"), 8"×6" (cover 6.5"×8.5"), 11"×14" (cover 11.5"×14.5") and A4 (cover 8.75"×12"). For anything else — 5"×7", 8"×10", 11"×17", A3 and more — order a Made-to-Fit cover with your exact measurements.

QIs there a minimum order or setup fee?

No. Order as few as one cover — there are no order minimums and no setup fees.

QDo you offer bulk or school pricing?

Yes. Bulk discounts apply automatically in your cart — just add the quantities you need. Most bulk orders are completed within 3–4 business days.

QAre diplomas or certificates included?

No. We sell covers and holders only — documents, diplomas and certificates are not included.

02 — Personalization

QWhat can you print on a cover?

A logo, seal, crest or custom text, foil-printed in Metallic Gold or Metallic Silver.

QCan I see my cover before I order?

Yes — the step-by-step builder shows your color, text, font and foil on a live preview as you go, and you review every choice before adding to cart. Our design team also reviews each order.

QWhich fonts can I choose?

Old English, Times New Roman, Arial and Cursive Script.

QWill my multi-color logo print in color?

No. Multi-colored logos are simplified to a single foil color for a high-quality print.

03 — Shipping & production

QHow fast will my order ship?

Production typically begins the next business day, and most orders are completed within 1–2 business days.

QHow much is shipping?

Orders over $35 ship free via UPS Ground. Under $35, choose your service at checkout. Every order includes tracking.

QI have a deadline — what should I do?

Include the date in your order notes at checkout, or email contact@wehonoru.com.

QCan I pick up my order?

Yes — pickup is available at HU HQ in Rockville, VA, usually ready in 2–4 days.

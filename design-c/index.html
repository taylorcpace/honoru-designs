FAQ

Good to know

What sizes do your covers come in?
Four standard sizes: 8.5"×11" Letter (cover 9"×11.5"), 8"×6" (cover 6.5"×8.5"), 11"×14" (cover 11.5"×14.5") and A4 (cover 8.75"×12"). For anything else — 5"×7", 8"×10", 11"×17", A3 and more — order a Made-to-Fit cover with your exact measurements.
Can I see my cover before I order?
Yes — the step-by-step builder shows your color, text, font and foil on a live preview as you go, and you review every choice before adding to cart. Our design team also reviews each order.
How fast will my order ship?
Production typically begins the next business day, and most orders are completed within 1–2 business days.
Are diplomas or certificates included?
No. We sell covers and holders only — documents, diplomas and certificates are not included.

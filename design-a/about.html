Ordering

What sizes do your covers come in?
Four standard sizes: 8.5"×11" Letter (cover 9"×11.5"), 8"×6" (cover 6.5"×8.5"), 11"×14" (cover 11.5"×14.5") and A4 (cover 8.75"×12"). For anything else — 5"×7", 8"×10", 11"×17", A3 and more — order a Made-to-Fit cover with your exact measurements.
Is there a minimum order or setup fee?
No. Order as few as one cover — there are no order minimums and no setup fees.
Do you offer bulk or school pricing?
Yes. Bulk discounts apply automatically in your cart — just add the quantities you need. Most bulk orders are completed within 3–4 business days. Schools and organizations can also request a quote or sign up for wholesale.
Are diplomas or certificates included?
No — certificates, awards and diplomas are not included. Our covers are designed to hold and protect your existing document.

Personalization

What can you print on a cover?
A logo, seal, crest or custom text, foil-printed in Metallic Gold or Metallic Silver. Upload your image file for reference — we can recreate most designs.
Will my multi-color logo print in color?
No. To ensure a high-quality print, multi-colored logos are simplified to a single foil color, which may slightly alter your design. A black-and-white version of your logo gives the best result.
Can I see my design before it is printed?
Our design team personally reviews and adjusts every layout. For a precise preview, add an Emailed Digital Mock-Up ($15.00): unlimited revisions, and we start production after you approve.
Which fonts can I choose?
Old English, Times New Roman, Arial and Cursive Script.

Shipping & production

How fast will my order ship?
Production typically begins the next business day, and most orders are completed within 1–2 business days. Custom orders may require additional time.
How much is shipping?
Orders over $35 ship free via UPS Ground. For orders under $35, choose your shipping service at checkout. Every order includes tracking.
I have a deadline — what should I do?
Include the date in your order notes at checkout, or email contact@wehonoru.com. We do our best to meet every deadline that is communicated to us.
Can I pick up my order?
Yes — pickup is available at HU HQ in Rockville, VA, usually ready in 2–4 days.

Still have a question?

For the quickest response, email us — our team is happy to help with sizing, artwork and deadlines.
